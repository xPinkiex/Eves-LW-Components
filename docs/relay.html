Eve's Normally-Closed Relay

A relay that connects its two pegs by default and disconnects them while its control input is high: the inverse of Logic World's relay.

Pegs

PegWhereWhat it does
0TopControl: low = closed (connected), high = open.
1Back faceThe switched connection. While closed, the two pegs are joined into one network: a signal passes both ways, as through a vanilla relay.
2Front face

The block is 1 wide, 1 high and 2 deep. Only a change of the control input makes it switch.

Useful where something must be connected unless told otherwise: a default bus driver, a reset path, or replacing a relay plus an inverter.
